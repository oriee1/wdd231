NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for nonprofit organizations that want to participate in the Abuja business community.

  • No annual membership fee
  • Access to chamber networking opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events
  • Community and nonprofit networking opportunities

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides essential resources for businesses beginning to expand their network.

  • All basic chamber networking opportunities
  • Business development resources
  • Access to selected training sessions
  • Discounts on selected chamber events

Silver Membership

Silver Membership provides additional promotional and educational opportunities for established businesses.

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Increased event discounts
  • Business advertising opportunities
  • Eligibility for selected member spotlights

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides the highest level of chamber visibility, networking, and promotional opportunities.

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Priority event promotion opportunities
  • Premium training and networking opportunities
  • Priority consideration for home page spotlights
  • Additional discounts on selected chamber events
